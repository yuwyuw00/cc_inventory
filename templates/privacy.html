Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

Columban College is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who use the Medical Inventory Management System. This policy outlines what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your username, email address, and activity logs within the system. Additionally, we collect item-related data for inventory purposes, including item name, category, stock levels, expiration dates, and descriptions.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

All data collected is used solely for managing and tracking medical inventory efficiently. It helps ensure accountability, improve record accuracy, and maintain security.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely in our internal database. We employ encryption, access controls, and regular backups to safeguard your data from unauthorized access or loss.

4. Access to Information

Only authorized personnel have access to user and inventory data. We do not share or sell your data to third parties.

5. User Rights

You have the right to request access to your information, correct inaccuracies, or request deletion of your data, subject to institutional policies and data retention requirements.

6. Cookies and Tracking

This system does not use third-party cookies or external tracking. However, your login sessions are tracked internally for security purposes.

7. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of any changes.
